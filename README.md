# CampusLoop

### Discover. Participate. Belong.

CampusLoop is a campus engagement platform designed to connect students with clubs, communities, and events while maintaining a reliable record of verified campus participation.

It provides a single platform where students can discover clubs, explore activities, join communities, register for events, verify attendance, and build their campus participation profile.

---

## 🚀 Overview

Campus activities are often scattered across different communication channels, making it difficult for students to discover opportunities and for clubs to manage participation.

CampusLoop brings these activities together into one platform.
